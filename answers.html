Questions people search after a lockout

These answers are free general information for consumers. They are not legal advice and not a substitute for checking a provider’s Texas license yourself.

Why did the bill not match the ad?

Ads often show a trip or starting figure. After-hours, mileage, and a new lock after drilling are commonly added later. Ask for one quoted total that includes those items before work starts.

Is a $25 trip fee a warning sign?

Working locksmiths on trade forums often treat a very low trip number as a bait listing. Treat any quote that is only a trip fee as incomplete.

Pick or drill?

Ask which they will try first. Drilling usually means you also buy a replacement cylinder. That is a different job than an open.

Maps pin with no shop site?

Google has publicly described fake locksmith listing rings. Prefer a company that publishes a phone on a site it controls, then confirm a Texas Department of Public Safety license number.

Do I pay before they arrive?

A small card hold is sometimes used to reduce no-shows. A large prepay before anyone is on site is a reason to call another shop.

After-hours pricing

Nights and weekends are often a different rate. Ask whether the quoted total already includes that add-on.

Car lockout vs house lockout

Automotive work can involve programming tools and a different price path than a house cylinder. Say the vehicle year and whether you have any working key.
