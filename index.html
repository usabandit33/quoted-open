Get a quoted total before they touch the lock

Emergency lockouts pull national call centers onto the first page of search. This page is a short checklist and phones copied from shops’ own sites.

  • Ask for a total before they start — trip fee plus the open, not a teaser number.
  • Ask whether they pick or drill, and whether a drilled lock is extra.
  • Ask for the Texas locksmith license number and a name you can look up.
  • Prefer a shop with its own website, not only a Google Maps pin.
  • Do not pay a large deposit before anyone arrives.
